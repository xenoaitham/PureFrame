# Legal Considerations

## Intended Use

PureFrame is designed for **private, personal use** on video files you legally own or have the legal right to modify.

## What PureFrame Does

- Processes video files locally on your machine
- Applies visual overlays (blurs) to flagged regions
- Creates a modified copy of the video file
- Operates entirely offline after initial model download

## What PureFrame Does NOT Do

- ❌ Does not bypass DRM or copy protection
- ❌ Does not download, stream, or redistribute video content
- ❌ Does not modify the original file (creates a new output file)
- ❌ Does not upload any data to the internet
- ❌ Does not provide any censorship certification or rating

## User Responsibility

- You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to process and modify the video files
- Modified output files are your responsibility - do not redistribute copyrighted content
- PureFrame's detections are AI-based and not guaranteed to catch all explicit content
- PureFrame is a tool, not a replacement for parental judgment or legal compliance

## Copyright Notice

PureFrame does not claim any ownership or modification rights over the content it processes. The software is a tool - the legal responsibility for how it is used rests with the user.

## Jurisdiction

Laws regarding video modification, content filtering, and fair use vary by country and region. The legality of creating a modified copy of a video you own depends on your jurisdiction. This software and its documentation do not constitute legal advice.
